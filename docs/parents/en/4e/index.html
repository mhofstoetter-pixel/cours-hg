How to help your child

  • Ask your child to explain the chapter to you in their own words, as if they were the teacher.
  • Ask them to say the vocabulary words and what they mean. They do not need to recite them word for word, but they must understand them.
  • Do a QCM together. It is not marked. Your child can try again as many times as they like.
  • Short and often is better than long and rarely: 10 minutes a few times a week is enough.
