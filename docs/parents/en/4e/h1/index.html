H1

Middle class, trade and slavery in the 18th century

What your child is learning

In the 18th century, the French ports on the Atlantic Ocean become very rich: Nantes, Bordeaux, La Rochelle.

They trade with the colonies in America. Ships bring back sugar, coffee and cotton.

These products are grown by African slaves, on large farms called plantations.

To get slaves, Europeans buy them in Africa: this is the slave trade (la traite). About 12 million Africans were taken by force to America.

This trade mostly makes the merchants of the ports rich. They are called the merchant middle class (bourgeoisie marchande).

Words to know

Colony (Colonie)
A territory conquered and ruled by a foreign country, which exploits it.
Trade (Négoce)
Buying goods in order to sell them again.
Merchant middle class (Bourgeoisie marchande)
The merchants and ship owners of the ports, who became very rich through trade.
Plantation (Plantation)
A large farm that grows only one crop, such as sugar cane, to sell it in Europe.
Slavery (Esclavage)
A system in which people belong to a master and are forced to work.
Slave trade (Traite)
The trade in slaves: captured Africans are sold and taken by force to America.
Triangular trade (Commerce triangulaire)
The three-stage journey of a slave ship: Europe, Africa, America, then back to Europe.
Black Code (Code noir)
A text by the king (1685) that sets the rules of slavery in the French colonies. The slave is treated as an object.

Key dates and places

  • 18th century: the Atlantic slave trade is at its highest point.
  • 1685: the Black Code (Code noir).
  • Nantes, Bordeaux, La Rochelle: major Atlantic ports.
  • Saint-Domingue (now Haiti), Martinique, Guadeloupe: French colonies in the Caribbean.

Help in 10 minutes

Ask your child these questions. Open the answer to check.

Why do the Atlantic ports become rich in the 18th century?Show the answer

Thanks to trade with the colonies in America: they sell the sugar, coffee and cotton again in Europe.

Who grows the sugar and coffee in the colonies?Show the answer

African slaves, forced to work on the plantations.

What is the slave trade?Show the answer

The trade in slaves: Africans are bought in Africa and taken by force to America to be sold there.

How many Africans were taken to America?Show the answer

About 12 million, between the 16th and the 19th century.

Who profits most from this trade?Show the answer

The merchants and ship owners of the ports, the merchant middle class. The planters and the king also profit.

What will be assessed

Being able to explain where the ports' wealth comes from, knowing the vocabulary words and presenting a document with the CANDI method.
