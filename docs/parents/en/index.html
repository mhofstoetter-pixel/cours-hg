Parents' area

This website contains your child's history, geography and civics lessons.

These are the same documents as on Éléa, the school's online platform. Here, you do not need an account or a password.

These pages are written for you. They explain what your child is learning and how to help, even if you never studied history or geography.

How to use this site

  1. Choose your child's class: 4e or 5e.
  2. Look at the chapter « in class right now ».
  3. Read the words to know with your child.
  4. Ask your child the questions in « Help in 10 minutes ». The answers are written so that you can check.
  5. The « Élève » (Pupil) button at the top of the page shows the website as your child sees it.

Practical questions

My child was absent. What should we do?Show the answer

All the lessons are on this website. Your child can read the lesson and copy it into their exercise book. They can also ask a classmate what was done in class.

Éléa is not working. What can we do?Show the answer

Use this website. It has the lessons, the BEX, the QCM and the investigations. It works on a phone, with no account and no password.

How do I contact the teacher?Show the answer

Write to the teacher through the Pronote messaging system. You cannot send messages from this website.

Does my child have to learn everything on the website?Show the answer

Above all, your child must know the vocabulary words and the key facts (dates, places) of each chapter. The BEX are for practising the methods.

Does the website record my child's results?Show the answer

No. This website does not send or record anything. The QCM and the ticked boxes stay only on the phone or computer being used.
